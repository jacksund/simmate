an open collective & full-stack framework for chemistry research

Our mission

Simmate's primary goal is to build a community that openly shares chemistry data and infrastructure. We believe that energy is wasted and research is slowed when academics repeatedly perform the same calculations in isolation, so we sought to build a platform for collaboration. This is why all data and workflow results are openly accessible to everyone in our public website. However, for the majority of researchers that work with confidential data, we still wish to collaborate through building the chemistry infrastructure together. For this reason, 100% of our codebase is open-source, and it is free to set up your own private server.

All data is free & open

Easy, unrestricted access to data is what enables new ideas and great science. After all, we can only truly innovate once we understand what has already been studied by others.

Compute is scalable & collaborative

The type of computational resources that researchers have access to varies greatly depending on if you are in academia, industry, or even just a hobbyist. Our infrastructure scales across the cloud, HPC, & personal computers to carryout calculations together.

Earnings are transparent & shared*

You can earn when you contribute computer resources for others to use. And to make everything as transparent & fair as possible, we make our financial data public - so you can trust that the science is coming first.

* payouts depend on several factors, such the demand created by other users and how much compute you provided. Make sure you read and understand our finances .

Popular chemistry datasets are built-in*

Many of the most widely used datasets in chemistry — such as the Materials Project, COD, and PubChem — are integrated into Simmate, so you can search across all of them in one place. Data from every source is cleaned and standardized, making it easy to bring into your own projects.

* We can only redistribute data when the original provider's license allows it. Some datasets can therefore only be used by installing Simmate locally and downloading the data directly from the provider.

Workflows are built on trusted software

There is an endless list of software to consider for chemistry calculations, and each program comes with its own inputs, outputs, and quirks. Simmate wraps popular, well-established codes — such as VASP, Quantum ESPRESSO, and Psi4 — into workflows that handle setup, catch and fix common errors, and scale from your laptop to HPC clusters. Explore our library of ready-to-use workflows, or build your own with our toolkit.

The codebase is 100% open-source

Simmate is built on the shoulders of open-source giants like pymatgen, RDKit, Django, PostgreSQL, and htmx. Our own code is released under the permissive BSD 3-Clause license, so every part of Simmate is free to install, use, and modify — whether for academic or commercial work. Run your own server, collaborate with us on the public website, or contribute directly on GitHub.
